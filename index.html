What is GC³?+

GC³ is Quantocos's Global Cloud Capability Center: monthly subscription pods that pair named human specialists with AI agents to run creative, digital marketing, strategy, interactive, automation and back-office work inside your own tools.

What does a pod cost?+

Work is priced in credits at $25 each. The Creative pod is $3,000 a month for 120 credits, Digital $6,000 for 240 and Strategy $9,000 for 360. Prices are published rather than quoted, and there is no setup fee and no exit fee.

How quickly does work start?+

Pods are staffed to overlap your business day and work inside your tools from the first week. Days 1–30 cover access, brand ingestion and the first brief.

What are the five gates?+

Every deliverable passes five sign-offs: G1 brief approval by the pod lead, G2 direction selection with the client, G3 internal QA, G4 client review with a 48 business-hour window, and G5 go-live approval from both the pod lead and the client. Nothing goes live without both signatures.

Who owns the work?+

Copyright and intellectual property in everything produced assign to you, and work, source files and IP stay with you when the engagement ends.

Is there a lock-in?+

Subscriptions run month to month. There is no setup fee and no exit fee, and the minimum term and notice period are stated in the engagement letter before you sign.

How are credits tracked?+

Balance and burn rate stay visible in the portal, and credits are drawn down as work moves.
