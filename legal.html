Privacy notice

We collect what you send us: the fields you complete in an enquiry form, and the email or telephone details you choose to give. If you use the site, our host records standard server logs — IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamp — for security.

We use enquiry data to answer you, and for nothing else. We do not sell it, we do not build advertising profiles from it, and we do not share it with third parties except where a contracted delivery partner needs it to respond in your market, or where the law requires it.

Client material — brand assets, data, documents — is held in the tenant it belongs to and is not used to train shared models. Access is controlled at tenant level with role-based permissions.

We keep enquiry data for two years and client material for the term of the engagement plus the retention period set out in the engagement letter. You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hello@quantocos.com. We answer those requests within thirty days.

Cookie settings

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. There is nothing to consent to and no banner, because there is nothing to ask about.

It stores one local preference in your browser: whether you have switched animation off. If you have, that choice is remembered so the site does not ask again. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

Fonts are served from Google Fonts, which receives the request for the font file. If you would prefer to avoid that, the build can be served with self-hosted fonts — ask us and we will provide them.

Legal & compliance

Accessibility

We target WCAG 2.2 level AA. The site is built to work with a keyboard alone, with a screen reader, at 200% zoom, and without JavaScript or without images.

Concretely: every interactive control is reachable and operable by keyboard, navigation panels open on focus and close on Escape, focus is always visible, color contrast is measured rather than estimated, and motion respects your system's reduced-motion setting. Choosing to switch animation off does not remove any content from the page.

If something here does not work for you, tell us what you were trying to do and what happened, at hello@quantocos.com. We treat an accessibility report as a defect and will tell you when it is fixed.

Modern slavery statement

Quantocos does not tolerate forced labour, debt bondage, human trafficking or the withholding of documents or wages anywhere in its operations or its supply chain.

We are a young company and we say plainly that we have no long track record of audits to point at. What we can state is the standard we hold: every engagement is with a named legal entity, every specialist is directly employed or contracted with written terms, and contracted delivery partners are required to meet the same standard as a condition of working with us.

Reports or concerns go to speakup@quantocos.com, which is read by the partners and no one else. We will publish our first formal statement once the company has completed a full financial year.
