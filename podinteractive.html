What it replaces

The hire or the supplier this stands in for

A specialist 3D or XR studio, and the coordination cost of briefing one separately from the brand and campaign work.

A product, a property or a process that cannot be understood from a photograph gets explained in prose, badly and at length. Interactive work fixes that, but most buyers cannot buy it: the studios that do it are priced for enterprise brands, and the agencies that understand the brand cannot build the thing.

Buying questions

What to settle before you subscribe

What kind of work does the Interactive Pod do?+

Real-estate and product visualisation, browser-based 3D configurators, scrollytelling microsites, VR showrooms, XR training modules and game-engine prototypes. Work is web-first with device and performance budgets agreed before the build.

Which tools and engines do you build with?+

The pipeline spans Spline, Figma, Framer, Webflow, Verge3D, 8th Wall, Godot, GDevelop and Blender, chosen per project rather than forced into one stack.

Can a 3D walkthrough run in a normal browser?+

Yes. Web-first delivery means the experience runs from a link without a headset or an app install, with a device and performance budget agreed up front so it stays usable on mid-range phones.

How does this relate to REMAU?+

REMAU is the real-estate specific pod: it includes interactive and XR work as part of a launch campaign. The Interactive Pod is the build capability on its own, for any sector.

How long does a configurator or walkthrough take?+

It is scoped from the asset inventory and the interaction list, then booked in sprints with a credit estimate per item. Nothing is built before you have seen the estimate.

Do you produce the underlying 3D assets?+

Yes, model production and optimization are part of the pod, as is retopology and asset compression for web delivery.
