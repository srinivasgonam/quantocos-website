Why one suite

One workspace, from brief to approval

A creative pod needs an image generator, a photo editor, a video editor, a sound editor and a 3D modeller. A growth pod needs an ad platform, a scheduler, a CRM and a reporting layer. A strategy pod needs somewhere to write. Normally that is twenty subscriptions and twenty logins.

Prism is the argument that the capability comes first and the tool second: one subscription covering the capabilities a specific business needs, in one environment, with one memory behind it.

The seven surfaces

What is inside Prism

Each surface is a working environment, not a widget. They share identity, memory and the automation layer, which is what makes them one product rather than seven.

Status: in development

Surface A synthetic media house

Studio

Generative image and video work plus the editors to finish it — image, video, audio and 3D in one interface instead of four applications and a file-transfer ritual.

  • AI image, video, 3D, music and background generation
  • Editing tools comparable to a photo editor
  • A video editor for cut, grade and finish
  • An audio editor for voice and sound design
  • Web-based 3D modeling
  • Local-first working, then sync the selections to Apara

See Studio on a live brief

Surface An engineering floor

Code

A development environment with the editor you already know, plus AI-assisted generation — and CoBridge so the agents working on different modules can talk to each other.

  • Monaco-based editor in the browser
  • AI-assisted code generation and review
  • CoBridge agent-to-agent messaging between modules
  • Custom bots and agents per task
  • Local development, then push to Apara for hosting
  • Micro-functions callable inside the build

See Code on a live brief

Surface A strategy hub

Plan

Where the thinking gets written down: wiki, mind maps, presentations, documents, spreadsheets and structured data — the planning surface a consulting pod actually works in.

  • Wiki and documentation
  • Mind maps and visual planning
  • Presentations and decks
  • Spreadsheets and structured data
  • Data storage and lightweight databases
  • Everything exportable and presentable

See Plan on a live brief

Surface Immersive production

Interactive

AR, VR, configurators and the interactive experiences that are half creative work and half software engineering.

  • AR and VR application development
  • Web-based 3D product configurators
  • Motion-led sites and scrollytelling
  • Game-engine prototyping
  • Spline, Figma, Framer, Webflow, Verge3D, 8th Wall, Godot, GDevelop and Blender workflows

See Interactive on a live brief

Surface A marketing studio

Martech

The digital marketing surface: search, paid, social, sentiment, CRM and messaging channels together, with the OSINT capability that most marketing stacks do not have.

  • SEO and answer-engine optimization
  • Paid advertising management
  • Social scheduling and sentiment analysis
  • CRM and email marketing
  • Broadcast across WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and messaging channels
  • OSINT and audience research

See Martech on a live brief

Surface An automation agency

Automate

Node-based agentic automation that can reach every other surface, because they are all part of the same system.

  • Node-based workflow building
  • Comparable in function to n8n, Langflow, CrewAI and AutoGen
  • Native integration with CoBridge
  • Automate Studio, Code, Plan, Interactive, Martech and Saltpetre
  • Third-party integrations and API work

See Automate on a live brief

Surface Signal from noise

Saltpetre

Feed it everything — URLs, articles, images, video — and it separates the signal from the noise, then turns that signal into datasets, MCPs, plugins and skills you can train on.

  • Ingest URLs, articles, images and video
  • Noise extraction and signal filtering
  • Build MCPs, plugins, skills and datasets from the signal
  • Composite data fragments into new modules
  • Fine-tune and train custom models on the datasets it produced

See Saltpetre on a live brief

How the surfaces connect

One system, so the handoffs disappear

Every surface can call the others. An automation built in Automate can drive Studio, write its output into Plan and hand the result to Code — because they are modules of the same product, not vendors with an integration contract.

Apara — where the output lives CoBridge — how the agents talk Sigma — what the system remembers
