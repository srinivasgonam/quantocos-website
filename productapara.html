Enterprise AI stalls at the point where every model, storage bucket and integration needs its own procurement, its own security review and its own tenant model. The estate grows faster than the governance around it.

What is Apara?+

Apara is Quantocos's cloud platform, in development. It supplies storage, LLM provision and routing, API and MCP hosting, CDN delivery, role-based access control and tenant management to every other product in the group.

Where does client data live?+

Client material is held in the tenant it belongs to and is not used to train shared models. Access is controlled at the tenant level with role-based permissions, and clients are told which regions their data is served from.

Do we have to move to Apara to work with Quantocos?+

No. The pods integrate with the systems a client already runs. Apara is the substrate for the group's own products; moving onto it is a direction, not a precondition.
