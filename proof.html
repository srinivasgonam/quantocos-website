Proof, answered

The questions a buyer asks when there are no case studies yet.

What this page can and cannot evidence, and how the numbers are published.

What can I verify before a call?+

The published prices, the 127-service credit matrix, the contract pack and the five gates every deliverable passes through. There are no client logos or case studies yet, and this page does not pretend otherwise.

Is the price the same for every client?+

Yes. Work is priced at $25 a credit: Creative is $3,000 a month for 120 credits, Digital $6,000 for 240 and Strategy $9,000 for 360. The numbers are published in the site schema rather than quoted per prospect.

Do unused credits roll over?+

No. Credits reset monthly and the balance does not carry into the next month.

What happens if a month runs over the pool?+

Overage is charged at the overage rate stated in the engagement letter. Because the credit estimate is accepted at brief approval, an overrun is a decision you make rather than an invoice you discover.
