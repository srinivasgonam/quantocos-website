Questions

The things to settle before you subscribe

What exactly is a credit?+

Credits are the unit the portal bills in. Each request is estimated in credits before a pod picks it up, and that estimate is visible to you. The Creative Pod includes 120 credits/month, the Digital Pod 240 and the Strategy Pod 360. You can spend credits across any catalog your pod includes, and unused credits do not roll over into the following month.

What happens if we use more than the included credits?+

You buy additional credits on the same terms, or move up a pod. Nothing is delivered without a credit estimate first, so an overrun is always a decision you made rather than an invoice you received.

Which software and licenses are included?+

All of it: design and creative software, marketing platforms, AI generation, stock libraries, fonts, and the pod management overhead. There are no add-ons, ever — the license problem most companies have is that this cost is spread across a dozen vendors, and here it is inside one subscription.

How fast is turnaround?+

Pods work in weekly or biweekly sprints. Each request is estimated in credits before it starts, and nothing is delivered without a named human's sign-off.

Can we start with one pod and add later?+

Yes. The stack is additive: Digital includes Creative, Strategy includes both. Most teams start with one pod, run a quarter, and then either move up or add a custom pod alongside it.

Do you work white-label for agencies and consultancies?+

Yes. The same pods can run under your brand for your clients, which is how agencies add capacity without hiring. The engagement is with us; the relationship stays yours.

Is any of this offshore body-shopping?+

No. A pod owns an outcome and a calendar, with a named manager. You are not buying a timesheet, and you are not managing the team.

Minimum term?+

Month to month for the three base pods. Custom pods and REMAU carry a three-month minimum because of the depth of the setup.
